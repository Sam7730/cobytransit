YOUR INFORMATION

Privacy, in plain language.

CobyTransit is an independent transit guide. It is not operated by SCPT or SUNY. The passenger map does not request your location or include advertising or analytics trackers.

Saved stops and offline schedules

Stops you save and schedules you choose to download stay in your browser. They are not sent to the project maintainer. You can clear them in the Rider guide under “Saved stops, offline schedules & privacy,” or through your browser’s site-data controls.

Offline schedules cannot check new cancellations. Current GPS reports and staff credentials are not saved in the offline cache.

Maps and hosting

Street tiles come from OpenStreetMap. Requests disclose your IP address, the website’s origin and the map area you are viewing. Turning Streets off stops further tile requests. See OpenStreetMap’s privacy policy.

The hosting provider may retain request logs and, when access is restricted, require sign-in and use authentication cookies. CobyTransit’s passenger features do not require a separate rider profile. Configured live-data services are accessed through the website’s server.

Website reports

Website feedback opens a draft GitHub issue for you to review and submit using your own account. It includes the selected route, stop or run and schedule version. Reports are public; do not include personal details. Opening the report form does not submit anything.

Optional staff and GPS tools

Staff access tokens stay in memory for the current tab. The separate driver test page asks for location permission only after an authorized user chooses to start sharing. It sends the device identifier, coordinates, accuracy and measurement time. Assigned vehicle positions may be shown to people who can access the site. Only the latest report per device is retained by the application; it does not maintain a GPS history database. Hosting logs are separate.

Passengers never need to enable device tracking to plan a trip. Actual buses appear only when authorized equipment or a provider is configured.

Questions or help

For website concerns, use the feedback option in the Rider guide. For transit reservations or current service, call SCPT dispatch at 518-234-0952. Do not send staff or device credentials in a report.
