A LITTLE HELP ALONG THE WAY

Your rider guide.

Practical information for travelling with Schoharie County Public Transportation.

01

Fares & payment

Routes 1, 2, 2A and 3 have a published $2 one-way fare. Reservation and commuter services have different fares. Bring exact change or check SCPT’s Token Transit options.

Student and faculty benefits depend on route, date and valid ID. Confirm eligibility before travelling.

Official fares & passes →
02

Reservations & accessibility

Call ahead for on-call stops, route deviations and demand-response rides. SCPT advertises wheelchair-accessible service; discuss boarding and pickup needs with dispatch.

For Route 5, reserve by noon the day before. Other service deadlines vary.

About CobyTransit & our data

CobyTransit is an independent project by Sohaib Khan. It is not an official county or SUNY service. Times are published schedules, not live predictions.

Live bus locations appear only when a provider is connected and fresh GPS readings are available. Check Map options for connection status.

Saved stops, offline schedules & privacy

No accounts, analytics or personal location requests. Saved stops and optional offline schedules stay in this browser. Offline copies cannot show new cancellations.

Street tiles load from OpenStreetMap, which receives your IP address, referrer origin and viewed area. Turn Streets off in the map to stop further tile requests. Hosting providers may retain request logs.
